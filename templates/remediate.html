Remediate a course cartridge

Upload a Common Cartridge (.imscc) from Canvas, D2L or Moodle and get back a copy with the mechanical accessibility problems fixed, a change log, and a list of what still needs a person.

What it will and will not do

  • It does not change a word of the course. The visible text of every page and PDF it touches is compared before and after, and a fix that would have moved a word is dropped for that file. The report says how much text was compared.
  • It fixes markup: the page language, table header scope, heading levels that skip, and a PDF's language, title and title-bar setting.
  • It does not write words for you. Image descriptions, captions, table headers and frame titles are listed as still owed, not invented.
  • It does not guess the language of the course. Give it below, or none is written.

Student work comes first

The cartridge is screened before anything else. If it carries what looks like student work, such as caption transcripts of recorded student reflections, submissions or grades, it is turned away and nothing is kept. Export the course again without media and without student content. A course export is whatever the instructor chose to include, and that is not always only the course design.

Before you import the result

Importing it into a course that is already being taught does not replace anything. Canvas creates new pages with new ids and orphans every assignment, module item and gradebook column attached to the old ones. The result is right for a new shell, a Commons package, an archival copy or a course being handed on. For a live course, use the change log to apply the changes by hand.
